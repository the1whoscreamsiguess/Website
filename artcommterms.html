ARTWORK COMMISSION TERMS

- "Commissioned work" is defined in these Terms as any image created by the Artist ("T1WS") and delivered to a Client in exchange for monetary compensation. By commissioning the Artist to create visual art, the Client agrees to these Terms in full.
- Violation of these terms will result in cancellation of ongoing commissions from the Client and blacklisting from future commissions.
- The Artist reserves the right to decline an incoming commission for any reason.

I. PAYMENT, DELIVERY, AND LEAD TIMES

- Payment will be carried out on the Ko-Fi website using the avenues made available by the Artist.
- Work will begin upon payment of the full price of the commission and delivery of acceptable reference material from the Client to the Artist.
- Once finished, the commissioned work will be delivered to the Client digitally through an avenue agreed upon by the Artist and Client.
- The commissioned work will be finished and delivered to the Client within a maximum of thirty (30) days of work beginning.

II. CANCELLATIONS AND REFUNDS

- The Artist or Client may cancel a commission for any reason at any time prior to the completion of the commissioned work.
- In the event of a cancellation prior to the Artist beginning work, the Client will receive a full refund.
- In the event of a cancellation while work is in progress, the Client will receive a partial refund based on the progress of the commissioned work.

III. REVISIONS

- The Artist will provide watermarked progress updates to the Client for approval throughout the process of creating the commissioned work. At least one (1) progress update will be provided for the sketch, flat color, and finished piece where applicable.
- The Client may request up to two (2) major revisions for no additional cost while the commissioned work is being sketched. Once the sketch is finalized and approved by the Client, a further three (3) minor revisions may be made within reason as defined by the Artist. Further revisions will incur additional fees at the discretion of the Artist.

- The Artist retains all rights to commissoned works. The finished work may be used or showcased by the Developer for any purpose seen fit.
